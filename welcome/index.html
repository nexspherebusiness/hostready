Your first 10 minutes

  1. Fill in Your place. Check in, WiFi, parking, rules and your favorite spots. It saves as you type.
  2. Open Welcome book. Press Print or save as PDF. Leave it on the table or send it to guests.
  3. Open House signs. Print all 12. The WiFi sign has a QR code guests can scan.
  4. Open Guest messages. Type the guest's name and copy each message into your guest chat.
Put it on your home screen.
iPhone: open the app in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android: open it in Chrome, tap the three dots, then Add to Home screen.
Your details stay on your device.
HostReady has no account. Use the same phone or computer to see your saved properties. Hosting on two devices? Fill in the place on each one.
